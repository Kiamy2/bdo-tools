# Changelog

## 2026-11-23
- `--when-stuck stop|continue` (default `stop`; library: `when_stuck` in `Evaluator`, `simulate_plan()`, `simulate()`; `bdo.horses.WHEN_STUCK`): a horse with fewer learned skills than wanted ones can't get the full set. `stop`: every plan stops spending once the horse is certainly stuck (while leveling: learned skills + level-ups left < wanted skills; at level 30: no cleanup). `continue` = the previous behavior (results identical). With `stop`, the full-set chance and the stuck share are unchanged, coupons used are lower, and the partial-goal columns count what stuck horses held when they stopped. The header states the rule.
- The three-criteria comparison: `ran out` renamed `unfinished`; every column is over all horses unless its name ends with `| not stuck` or `| successes` (said in the header; those two columns now come last).

## 2026-11-22
- `--optimize`: the suggested plans are rechecked with 20,000 fresh horses (plain Python: 4,000): the tied plans of each budget, the rule's plan and at30; the suggestion is made on these numbers (the best of the search can be up to about 1 point too high on the horses it was picked on). Among plans within 0.3 points of the best full-set chance, the best is the one most likely to miss at most 1 wanted skill (`suggested_plan(..., safe_margin)`). `--coupons-used` and the extra columns use the same horses.
- `--optimize` compares plans on three criteria, by default (`--no-frontier` to skip): the full set within N coupons, the coupons used on average, and the stuck share (horses reaching level 30 with fewer learned skills than wanted ones); also the averages over the horses not stuck and over the successes, the median and 'at most B'. The plans no other plan beats on all three within noise (`--frontier-tol`), at most `--frontier-rows`, sorted by `--prefer chance|coupons|safe`; rechecked with `--recheck-runs` fresh horses. Shares never shown as 0% or 100% unless certain. Replaces the short-lived `--fewest-coupons`. Library: `Evaluator.plans()` results have `stuck`, and `used_ok` with `stuck=True`; `simulate_plan()` too; both `optimize()` take a `stage1` dict; `bdo.horses.frontier()`, `frontier_candidates()`, `FRONTIER_TOL`; `horses_fast.search_grid()`.
- Among plans tied on the full set and on "missing at most 1" (0.3 points), the suggested best is the one using the fewest coupons (a special T8 at 6 coupons: no swaps, 98.75% and 0.80 coupons, instead of V2, 98.76% and 2.34).
- The general rule's plan isn't suggested when a plan at least as likely to give the full set uses 0.2+ fewer coupons (`suggested_plan(..., cheaper)`, source 'cheaper'), nor for special or `--horse-file` horses, or more learned skills than level - 1 (a warning).
- `--horse-file` headers `tier`, `type`, `innate`.
- `docs/horse_skills_guide.md`: for AI assistants guiding a player through a horse's leveling (setup, what to ask, the commands, the per-level-up rules, being reliable with the numbers); linked from `AGENTS.md`, the README and the tool doc.
- `--innate SKILLS`: a special horse = a known horse with extra innate skills (out of its draw, same 'no skill' weight; `Horse.with_innate()`). Check it with `--check` and a reading.
- `--optimize --coupons-used [N,N,..]`: for the suggested plans of these budgets (default all), the chance of the full set with at most B coupons in total for every B (the plan followed as planned while leveling, then the level-30 cleanup until the full set), and the coupons used on average, median, 90% and 99%. `Evaluator.plans(at_most=True)` and `simulate_plan()` return `at_most[B]` (exact cleanup in the fast engine).
- The `--optimize` suggested plans show `used` (expected coupons used with that budget) and `per full set` (used / full-set chance); the fast `optimize()` returns `used`.
- Plans are printed in their effective form for the horse (`effective_plan()`; `plan_name()` and `describe_plans()` take `learned_count` and `start_level`): a `coursier_switch` threshold already reached is V2, a start level already passed is the next level.
- The header shows the horse as typed when it's an alias ("T8 Type 6 (an alias of T8 Type 4: the same data)", `typed_name()`), the learned skills in the `--lang` language with the in-game count ("15 + 1 innate (Sprint) = 16 in game"), and says when every skill left to learn is wanted (`sure_hits()`). Innate or unlearnable skills in `--learned` are ignored with a note.

## 2026-11-21
- The general rule now depends on the target set (C43, checked over 148 courser and 60 travel situations): 1 coupon: all at level 30; 2-24: V2 from level max(10, 31 - 1.75 x N), keep 1 (courser set with 2-6 coupons: from level 30 - 2 x N); 25+: from level 10, V0 until 12 learned skills, then V2, spending at most 24 while leveling. `rule_plan(budget, start_level, wanted)`, `rule_target()`; `suggested_plan(..., wanted)`.
- Named target sets: `--wanted courser` (what a T8 needs for courser training: Charge, Drift, Sprint, Instant Accel, Sideways, S: Instant Accel, S: Sideways) and `--wanted travel`; the tool's default is `courser` for a T8 and `travel` otherwise, and the output says which set it used. Library functions keep `TRAVEL_SET` as their default (`COURSER_SET`, `WANTED_SETS`, `default_wanted_set()` added).
- T8 types in the data file: `T8 Type 2` (also 7), `3`, `4` (also 6, 9), `5`, `19` (also 21), `20`, `22`, with weights from bdolytics and 'no skill' weights from in-game simulator readings (they differ by type: 30.0 to 48.5); the T8 example is also `T8 Type 1` / `Choky`. Horses can have `aliases`; `--list` shows them.
- The general rule (`rule_plan`, the `--optimize` suggested plans) refitted with the points reset, as a compact rule: 1 coupon: all at level 30; 2-19: V2 from level max(10, 31 - 1.75 x N), keep 1; 20-21: from level 11, V0 until 10 learned skills, then V2, keep 1; 22+: from level 10, V0 until 12 learned skills, then V2, spending at most 24 while leveling. `RULE_FITTED` is gone. Below 5 coupons the old rule spent every coupon while leveling (up to 14 points lost); the new one keeps 1.
- `--always-best` (with `--optimize`): suggest the best plan found for every budget, not the rule's plan when it's within 1 point.
- V0 (`coursier`, and the V0 phase of `coursier_switch`) swaps Fore Chop first, then Hind Kick: slightly better when Hind Kick is the heavier. `CLASSIC_FODDER` is now `["Fore Chop", "Hind Kick"]`; `coursier_v2` still cycles the heavier one, Hind Kick on ties.
- `Evaluator.plans(used=True)` and `simulate_plan()` return `used[B]`: the expected number of Change Coupons used with B coupons available (while leveling, then the level-30 cleanup until the set is complete or it can't go on). Exact in the fast engine (one more value in the cleanup sweep, `cleanup_values(used=True)`).
- Skill Change Points now go back to 0 when a Change Coupon gives the desired skill (seen in-game twice; before, the model let them grow by 1 per coupon). Both engines, while leveling and in the level-30 cleanup; `simulate_plan`'s `points_reset` option is gone (always on). Chances drop in hard cases, e.g. the T8 example at Skilled 1, best plan with 20 coupons: 61% instead of 71%.
- `bdo/horses_fast.py`: the exact level-30 cleanup is swept by wanted skills held, then points (the reset breaks the old order by points); `cleanup_values` lost its `bound` argument. `--optimize` on the T8 example: about 20 s / 0.5 GB at 20 coupons, 45 s / 1.2 GB at 40 (`--low-memory`: 0.8 GB).

## 2026-11-18
- `--optimize` ends with the average learned skills per level for the suggested plans (`Evaluator.learned_by_level`, `bdo.horses.learned_by_level`).
- `--optimize --selection-coupon planned|fallback`: a Mount Skill Selection Coupon used at the very end on the last missing skill (needs an unwanted skill to change). `planned`: every Change Coupon aims at the second rarest missing skill, keeping the rarest for it, and the plan search includes it; `fallback`: plans as without it, plus the chance if you decide at level 30 to use it. With `--tickets`, the suggested plans also show the chance if you keep the tickets. `simulate_plan(coupon=...)`, `Evaluator.plans(coupon=...)`.
- Bad cases shown: the strategies table has `<=1 missing` and `worst 5%` columns; the `--optimize` suggested plans show the chances of missing at most 1 / 2 wanted skills and what the worst 5% of horses miss (exact in the fast engine: `Evaluator.plans(tails=True)`; `simulate_plan` returns `tails`).
- `--optimize`: a suggested plan per budget after the table: the general rule's plan (`bdo/horses.py` `rule_plan`, fitted over 72 situations) when it's within 1 point of the best plan found, else the best. Plans are written as "from lvl 10, V0 until 11 learned skills, then V2, keep 5".
- `bdo/horses_fast.py`: the exact level-30 cleanup is now computed per batch of plans, sweeping the reachable states by Skill Change Points (two levels in memory; only points + coupons left <= starting points + largest budget). Same results; `--optimize` on the T8 example: 13 s / 0.5 GB instead of 30 s / 1.0 GB at 20 coupons, 26 s / 0.7 GB instead of 90 s / 2.9 GB at 40. New `--low-memory` (16-bit values). `Evaluator.plans()` evaluates a batch; the persistent `Table` is gone.
- `horse_skills.py`: new strategy `coursier_switch`: `coursier` swaps until the horse has `--switch-at` learned skills (default 12; innate ones don't count), then `coursier_v2` swaps. Building skills first, then stopping the loop, beats both with many coupons. `--optimize` searches its threshold (9-16); plans are now (strategy, start level, spending cap, threshold) in `bdo/horses.py` and `bdo/horses_fast.py`, and tied plans read "from lvl 7-11, V0 until 10-13 learned skills, then V2, keep 0-10".
- `horse_skills.py`: new strategy `coursier_v2`: swap only Hind Kick (the heavier fodder skill) and keep Fore Chop. Plain `coursier` loses many coupons to a Hind Kick <-> Fore Chop loop; `coursier_v2` needs far fewer coupons for the same chance in most cases (see the report).
- Level-30 cleanup rule changed to "swap the rarest unwanted skill held" (was: keep re-swapping the same slot). Slightly better, and as good as the best possible cleanup for the full set. Simulated numbers move by about +1 point.
- `--optimize N`: best plan per budget (strategy, start level, coupons kept), with tied ranges and coupons needed for 50/80/90/95%. `--target`: coupons needed per strategy. `--swap-from`: level to start swapping.
- New optional fast engine `bdo/horses_fast.py` (numba, `requirements-optional.txt`): every plan tried, level-30 cleanup computed exactly. `--engine auto|python|numba`; without numba everything still works in plain Python.
- `data/horse_skills.json`: Mythical Diné weights refined to 4 decimals from an in-game simulator reading (bdolytics rounds to 0.01); checked against a coupon-window reading at 2 Skill Change Points.

## 2026-11-16
- `--lang` in `items.py` and `prices.py` now takes several languages (`--lang en,fr`): names are matched in any of them and printed one column per language. Useful to translate item names, or to work in French end to end.
- Looser name matching in both tools: if there's no exact (case-insensitive) match, accents, `œ`/`oe`, typographic apostrophes and extra spaces are ignored, then all spaces and punctuation. A loose match that hits different names is refused with the candidates listed.
- Better suggestions for unmatched names: names containing every word in any order, across all `--lang` languages.
- An unknown `--lang` now stops at once with `UNKNOWN LANGUAGE` (exit 2). Before, `prices.py` reported every item as UNKNOWN ID. Arsha HTTP 400 errors are no longer retried.
- `prices.py` always fetches market data with `lang=en`; displayed names come from the item lists.
- Output is always UTF-8 (avoids crashes on Windows pipes with non-Latin names).
